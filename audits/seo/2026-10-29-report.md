- **Stack:** Next.js 16 Pages Router, static export → Cloudflare Workers static assets
- **Ad status:** ad-free (AdSense abandoned 2026-07-09, Nintendo IP; GA4 removed `7da08ec`). The ad and consent scopes are N/A.
- **Locales:** EN + FR (`/fr` subtree), 1,202 URLs each
- **Audited target:** live prod https://my-pokedex.com. **Build marker verified:** `/sw.js` reports `VERSION = "62cca2b"`, which is repo `master` HEAD (`62cca2b`, "fix(identity): drop Organization JSON-LD and the site © line"). ### robots-prod-vs-repo
