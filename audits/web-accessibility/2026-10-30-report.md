## Harness (this run)
Playwright 1.64.1 Chromium plus `@axe-core/playwright` (axe-core 4.14.0), run from a scratch dir outside the repo against **live prod**, with service workers blocked so every response comes from the network.
- **Contrast, real rendered background.** 3 routes (`/`, `/fr`, `/pokemon/pikachu`) × light and dark × 1280×900 and 360×780, at deviceScaleFactor 2 with transitions off. Dark mode was set with the real theme toggle (inside the Options sheet on mobile) and confirmed via `data-theme="dark"`.
  - **Placeholder:** glyph-pixel method. The field was screenshotted with the placeholder visible, then again with `::placeholder{color:transparent}`. Each changed pixel was compared with the same pixel in the second shot, so every glyph pixel is measured against its true composited background. 1,310–2,931 glyph pixels per case. Computed `::placeholder` colour and opacity were also read.
  - **Borders:** the 1 CSS-px border band sampled from screenshot pixels at the top and bottom centre of the search pill and the sort `<select>`, against the pixel 3 px outside (the real background) and 4 px inside (the fill). The mobile search was measured with the Options sheet closed, and the mobile sort select **inside the open Options sheet at 360 px** (`aria-expanded="true"`, screenshots checked). Computed border, fill and ancestor-composited background were recorded as a cross-check.
- **axe regression pass:** tags `wcag2a, wcag2aa, wcag21a, wcag21aa, wcag22a, wcag22aa` on `/`, `/fr`, `/pokemon/pikachu`, `/fr/privacy` and `/privacy` × light/dark × 1280×900 and 320×640, plus the mobile Options sheet open on `/` and `/fr` in both themes. That is **24 runs**.
- **FR footer:** rendered footer links and `aria-current` on 8 FR and 6 EN routes. A real click-through of each of the 4 FR footer links from `/fr` (client-side navigation), checking landing path, `<h1>`, `lang`, `aria-current` and HTTP status. The raw (no-JS) footer `href`s were checked on all 2,404 sitemap pages by the SEO BFS crawl of the same day.

## Definition of Done — per check

### axe-violations
**RE-RUN (targeted).** PASS. **0 violations** on all 24 runs: 5 routes × 2 themes × 2 viewports, plus the Options sheet open on `/` and `/fr` × 2 themes.
- Method note: a first dark-theme pass that ran axe 300 ms after the theme toggle reported `color-contrast` on the nav tabs (`.DIaAYa_tabActive`, `.DIaAYa_bottomTab…`). Those elements carry `transition: background-color 1s ease-in-out`, and their computed backgrounds were mid-fade at that moment (e.g. `rgb(201,202,205)` → `rgb(255,255,255)`). Re-run 3 s after the toggle, they settle and axe reports 0. This is a sampling artifact, not a defect, and it does not come from the fix commits (the nav CSS is untouched).

The full 10-29 coverage (15 routes × 2 viewports, 7 states, target-size runs) is carried forward. Neither fix commit touches markup or styles outside the search field, the sort select and the footer `href`s.

|---|---|---|---|---|---|

### ui-contrast
**RE-RUN.** PASS (**was finding #2**). Measured from screenshot pixels: the border band vs the pixel 3 px outside and 4 px inside, at top and bottom centre.

| Control | Where | Theme | Border (rendered) | vs outside | vs fill |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Search pill | header, 1280 and 360 (`/`, `/fr`, `/pokemon/pikachu`) | light | `rgb(125,134,153)` | **3.66:1** (on `#fff`) | **3.29:1** (on `#f1f3f8`) |

| Sort `<select>` | **mobile Options sheet, 360 px, open** (`/`, `/fr`) | light | `rgb(125,134,153)` | **3.66:1** (sheet `#fff`) | **3.29:1** |
